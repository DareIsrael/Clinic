Your questions, answered

Before you book

A few helpful things to know so you can choose the right appointment.

Is Dr. Fagbolagun accepting new patients?

No, Dr. Fagbolagun is not accepting new patients. New patients can use Dr. Reginald’s new appointment option

What happens at a new-patient appointment?

You'll have an opportunity to meet Dr. Reginald, discuss your health needs and learn about next steps for ongoing care. Booking this appointment does not automatically establish a family-physician relationship or guarantee enrolment.

I was a patient of Dr. Okwechime. What should I book?

You can book a continuing-care appointment for a current medical need. You may also choose to meet Dr. Reginald if you'd like to explore having him as your family doctor. This is your choice; there is no automatic reassignment.

Are same-day appointments guaranteed?

No. Same-day appointments depend on available clinicians and clinic capacity. Walk-in visits are intended for time-sensitive, non-emergency concerns. If you're unsure, call reception for guidance.

Can walk-in physicians complete forms or renew controlled medications?

These services cannot be guaranteed. Complex forms may require your records, a full assessment and an ongoing physician-patient relationship. All prescription decisions are made by the physician following an appropriate assessment.

What if I need emergency medical care?

For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Online bookings and clinic telephone messages are not monitored for emergencies.
